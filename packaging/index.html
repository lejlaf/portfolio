How I work

A process built around the dieline.

  1. 01

    Brief & shelf audit

    The product, the retailer, the customer, and the competitors sitting either side of it on the shelf.

  2. 02

    Directions

    Distinct concepts, shown on the real dieline from day one so nobody falls for something that won't fold.

  3. 03

    System

    The chosen direction becomes rules: grid, hierarchy, color per SKU. The next flavor becomes fast and consistent.

  4. 04

    Production

    Artwork built and preflighted on the manufacturer's dieline. I'm used to answering file questions directly with factories.

About

Hi, I'm Layla. I design the box and what goes on it.

I've spent more than twenty years in graphic design across agencies, IT firms and publishing houses. Today I focus on packaging, labels and the brand systems behind them.

I illustrate much of my own work, so the artwork and the structure are designed together instead of being stitched together at the end. I've worked directly with manufacturers in China on file preparation, and I treat a clean, correctly built production file as part of the design, not an afterthought.

I'm based in Sarajevo and work remotely with brands from Munich to Quito.
