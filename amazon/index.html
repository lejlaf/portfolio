How I work

Built for a team, not a solo show.

  1. 01

    Brief & templates

    I start from the brand's guidelines and existing templates, and ask the questions early.

  2. 02

    Master artwork

    One carefully built master per asset, layered so text, language and size changes are quick.

  3. 03

    Adapt & QA

    Resizes and language versions checked against specs, copy and the brand before anything leaves my desk.

  4. 04

    Deliver & amend

    Named, organized files. Feedback rounds handled fast and without drama.

About

Hi, I'm Layla. I like the details nobody notices until they're wrong.

I'm a senior graphic designer with more than twenty years across agencies, IT firms and publishing houses. For the Amazon brands Milula and RYVE I designed products such as journals, coloring books and calendars, and prepared listing images, working in constant collaboration with the team.

I also communicated directly with manufacturers in China about file preparation and checked that every file was set up correctly before it went to print.
