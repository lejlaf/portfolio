Reflect, Overcome & Grow

Three coloring books, one visual world. Every page drawn by hand, and a set of rules that kept all three volumes speaking the same language.

01

The brief

A three-book coloring set, each volume built around one theme: reflecting, overcoming and growing. The set had to work as a journey you move through in order, and as three books that each stand alone.

What the client asked for, who the buyer is, page count per book, trim size.

02

The challenge

  1. A

    Volume

    A lot of original pages, all hand-drawn, with no drop in quality from the first to the last.

  2. B

    Consistency

    Three themes that should feel distinct but unmistakably part of one set.

  3. C

    Colorability

    Line art that is calming to color: clear shapes, the right density, nothing too fiddly.
