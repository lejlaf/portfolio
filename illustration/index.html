About

Hi, I'm Layla. I draw things that end up in people's hands.

I'm a senior graphic designer and illustrator with more than twenty years across agencies, IT firms and publishing houses. My illustration is drawn from scratch, in Illustrator and Procreate, and I'm drawn most to botanical and nature subjects.

Most of my recent work has been product design for Amazon brands: coloring books, journals, card decks and calendars, developed alongside project managers and prepared directly with manufacturers. I also sell my own designs as a Canva Official Creator and on Etsy and Zazzle.
